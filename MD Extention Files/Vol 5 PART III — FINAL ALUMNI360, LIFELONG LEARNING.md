# **CHAPTER 286**

# **ALUMNI360 CONSTITUTION**

Graduation shall not end the relationship between the learner and the institution.

Alumni360 shall establish the governed lifelong relationship layer connecting:

- graduates;

- professionals;

- entrepreneurs;

- researchers;

- mentors;

- employers;

- industry experts;

- partners;

- investors;

- global ambassadors;

- future learners.

## **286.1 Alumni360 Purpose**

Alumni360 shall help the institution:

- maintain accurate alumni relationships;

- understand graduate outcomes;

- support career development;

- connect alumni with students;

- connect alumni with employers;

- create mentorship opportunities;

- develop global chapters;

- support entrepreneurship;

- identify investment opportunities;

- create institutional partnerships;

- expand the DAIOS marketplace;

- strengthen lifelong learning;

- measure long-term educational impact.

## **286.2 Alumni360 Domains**

# **MICRO-CREDENTIAL AND STACKABLE LEARNING CONSTITUTION**

Microcredentials shall represent governed, verifiable learning.

## **297.1 Microcredential Requirements**

Every microcredential must define:

- Credential ID;

- title;

- learning outcomes;

- target learners;

- level;

- workload;

- assessment;

- evidence;

- faculty or provider;

- validity;

- quality approval;

- stacking pathway;

- credential format.

## **297.2 Stackable Pathway**

Approved credentials may stack toward:

- specialization;

- professional certificate;

- diploma;

- degree credit where formally permitted;

- career pathway;

- industry certification.

## **297.3 Assessment Integrity**

Microcredentials shall not be issued solely for:

- attendance;

- video completion;

- registration;

- payment

unless the credential is clearly labelled as participation rather than assessed competency.

## **297.4 Credential Portability**

Credentials should be:

- digitally verifiable;

- portable;

- tamper-resistant;

- linked to evidence where appropriate;

- accessible to the learner.

## **297.5 Credential Expiry**

Credentials involving rapidly changing skills may include:

- issue date;

- renewal period;

- continuing-practice requirement;

- reassessment.
