# **CHAPTER 187**

# **ENTERPRISE KPI DICTIONARY**

No dashboard may exist without governed KPI definitions.

Every KPI used within DAIOS shall have one official definition, one approved formula, one accountable owner and one authoritative source.

## **187.1 Mandatory KPI Metadata**

Every KPI must record:

- KPI ID;

- Official KPI Name;

- Purpose;

- Domain;

- Definition;

- Formula;

- Numerator;

- Denominator;

- Unit;

- Direction of Improvement;

- Data Source;

- Data Owner;

- KPI Owner;

- Target;

- Baseline;

- Threshold;

- Review Frequency;

- Reporting Frequency;

- Segmentation;

- Effective Date;

- Version;

- Approval Status.

## **187.2 One KPI, One Meaning**

The same KPI name shall not be used with multiple uncontrolled definitions.

Where different calculations are required, they must have:

- different KPI IDs;

- clearly different names;

- documented business purpose;

- approved relationship.

## **187.3 KPI Classes**

DAIOS KPIs may be classified as:

- Strategic;

- Enterprise;

- Governance;

- Product;

- Delivery;

- Architecture;

- AI;

- Security;

- Knowledge;

- Revenue;

- Customer;

- Marketplace;

- Student;

- Faculty;

- Research;

- Startup;

- Alumni;

- Talent;

- Asset;

- Infrastructure;

- Sustainability;

- Operational.

## **187.4 Leading and Lagging Indicators**

Every major outcome should identify:

- leading indicators;

- lagging indicators;

- risk indicators;

- control indicators.

## **187.5 KPI Versioning**

A KPI formula change requires:

- change reason;

- impact analysis;

- historical-comparison note;

- owner approval;

- effective date;

- version record.

Historical KPI results shall not be silently recalculated without disclosure.

# **CHAPTER 188**

## **188.1 KPI Owner Responsibilities**

## **188.2 Data Owner Responsibilities**

- correction.

## **188.3 KPI Without Owner**

## **188.4 Target Accountability**

- accountable owner;

- contributing owners;

- target period;

- baseline;

- assumptions;

- dependencies;

- approved changes.

## **188.5 Shared Contribution**

Multiple teams may contribute to one KPI, but final accountability must remain clear.

- executive performance;

- inclusion criteria;

- exclusion criteria

shall be treated as a governance violation.
