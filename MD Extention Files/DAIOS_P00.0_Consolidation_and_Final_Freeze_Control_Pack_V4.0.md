## 1.2 Harmonized Chapter Numbering Rule

- Chapter numbers are globally unique across the complete Constitution and shall never restart within a volume or part.

- Chapter numbers 1–402 constitute the Version 4.0 constitutional baseline.

- A new chapter added before the next major edition shall use an insertion code such as 223A only when renumbering would break published references; normal preference is to add at the end of the relevant reserved anchor through a formal amendment.

- Parts and sections are organizational labels and do not alter chapter identity.

- Clauses use the format Volume.Chapter.Clause in machine-readable records, while published text may retain Chapter.Section numbering.

- No chapter number may be reused after repeal; the number remains reserved and is marked Repealed or Superseded.

## 2.1 Deduplication Rules

- Authoritative ownership: one chapter owns the definition, formula or control.

- Domain implementation: another chapter may define a domain-specific application without restating the baseline.

- Cross-reference instead of repetition: repeated mandatory text shall be replaced with a reference during final editorial consolidation.

- Controlled aliases: ambiguous repeated labels shall receive domain qualifiers.

- No loss of safeguards: deduplication shall not remove a domain-specific ethical, legal, safety or human-rights control.
