## **31.1 Product Development Philosophy**

Products shall be:

- customer-driven;

- user-centered;

- outcome-driven;

- AI-enabled where valuable;

- secure by design;

- API-first;

- integration-ready;

- configurable;

- reusable;

- scalable;

- observable;

- documented;

- commercially or institutionally sustainable;

- globally benchmarkable;

- continuously improving.

## **31.2 Product Versus Custom Project**

- reusable modules;

- configurable workflows;

- standard templates;

- product features;

- marketplace assets;

- knowledge assets.

The system must understand the current reality before proposing the future state.

## **33.2 Mandatory Diagnostic Outputs**

P01 Intake and Diagnosis shall generate:

- Current-State Assessment;

- pain-point map;

- process map;

- complaint taxonomy;

- queue analysis;

- manual tracker inventory;

- documentation gap;

- stakeholder map;

## **33.3 Unclear Request Rule**

If the requesting unit cannot define its problem, the initiative shall remain in diagnosis.

# **CHAPTER 34**

# **SEARCH, REUSE AND NON-DUPLICATION CONSTITUTION**

Before proposing new development, DAIOS must execute:

**Search First
→ Reuse Second
→ Configure Third
→ Integrate Fourth
→ Extend Fifth
→ Build New Last.**

## **34.1 Search Scope**

Search must include:

- existing DAIOS products;

- existing modules;

- existing APIs;

- workflows;

- prompts;

- agents;

- datasets;

- dashboards;

- reports;

- student projects;

- faculty research;

- past competitions;

- archived systems;

- open-source solutions;

- commercial products;

- approved reusable components.

## **34.2 Duplication Classifications**

A proposed initiative shall be classified as:

- exact duplicate;

- functional duplicate;

- partial overlap;

- extension;

- replacement;

- integration opportunity;

- genuinely new product.

## **34.3 Duplicate Development Rule**

Duplicate development may proceed only when documented evidence demonstrates:

- legal necessity;

- security necessity;

- material architecture incompatibility;

- customer isolation requirement;

- strategic product differentiation;

- unacceptable dependency risk;

- approved replacement strategy.

Personal preference or lack of awareness is not valid justification.
