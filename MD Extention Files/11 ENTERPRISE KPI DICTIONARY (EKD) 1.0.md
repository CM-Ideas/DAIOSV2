# **DAIOS ENTERPRISE KPI DICTIONARY (EKD) 1.0**

## **Daffodil AI Operating System**

### **Enterprise KPI Governance, Measurement & Intelligence Standard**

# **1. EXECUTIVE DECLARATION**

The KPI Dictionary is the Measurement Constitution of DAIOS.

Without KPI definitions:

- Product Team measures one way

- Revenue Team measures another way

- AI Team uses different logic

- Dashboards show conflicting numbers

- Decision Intelligence becomes unreliable

The KPI Dictionary ensures:

### **One Definition**

### **One Formula**

### **One Data Source**

### **One Owner**

### **One Version Of Truth**

for every KPI across DAIOS.

# **2. KPI GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLE**

Every KPI must have:

### **KPI ID**

### **KPI Name**

### **Definition**

### **Formula**

### **Data Source**

### **Owner**

### **Frequency**

### **Target**

### **Threshold**

### **Dashboard**

No KPI may exist without a definition.
