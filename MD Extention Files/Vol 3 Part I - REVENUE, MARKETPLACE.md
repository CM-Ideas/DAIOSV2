# **CHAPTER 78**

# **REVENUE-FIRST CONSTITUTION**

## **78.1 Executive Principle**

A product without measurable value is an activity.

A product with value but no adoption is an experiment.

A product with adoption but no sustainable funding is a dependency.

A product with sustainable revenue or measurable institutional return is an asset.

Every commercial product must answer:

- Who is the customer?

- What problem does the product solve?

- Why will the customer pay?

- How much will the customer pay?

- How will the customer buy?

- How long will the customer remain?

- What will cause renewal?

- What will cause expansion?

- What will create profit?

- What will justify global scale?

## **78.2 Revenue or Institutional Value**

Not every DAIOS solution must immediately generate direct external revenue.

However, every solution must define one or more forms of value:

- direct revenue;

- recurring revenue;

- cost reduction;

- time saving;

- productivity improvement;

- risk reduction;

- student success;

- employee productivity;

- customer retention;

- institutional reputation;

- regulatory compliance;

- research impact;

- intellectual property;

- strategic capability.

An internal product without direct revenue must still demonstrate measurable institutional return.

## **78.3 Revenue Before Activity**

Commercial activity shall not be measured primarily through:

- number of calls;

- number of meetings;

- number of posts;

- number of campaigns;

- number of proposals;

- number of presentations.

Commercial performance shall be measured through:

- qualified demand;

- conversion;

- revenue;

- recurring revenue;

- gross margin;

- retention;

- customer success;

- expansion;

- profitability;

- strategic value.

## **78.4 Revenue Approval Rule**

No commercial product shall receive final launch approval without:

- identified customer;

- value proposition;

- pricing or approved value model;

- revenue forecast;

- cost model;

- customer acquisition plan;

- customer success plan;

- profitability path;

- accountable Revenue Owner.
