# **CHAPTER 99**

# **PRODUCT PROFITABILITY ENGINE**

Revenue alone is not commercial success.

A product may generate revenue and still destroy value through excessive:

- development cost;

- infrastructure cost;

- AI cost;

- implementation cost;

- customization;

- support;

- marketing;

- sales;

- discounts;

- partner commissions;

- unpaid receivables;

- operational complexity.

Every commercial product shall therefore operate under the Product Profitability Engine.

## **99.1 Product Profitability Purpose**

The Product Profitability Engine shall determine:

- whether the product creates sustainable financial value;

- which customers and plans generate margin;

- which costs are increasing;

- when the product reaches breakeven;

- whether pricing is adequate;

- whether AI and infrastructure costs are controlled;

- whether further investment is justified;

- whether the product should scale, correct, merge, reposition or retire.

## **99.2 Minimum Profitability Structure**

Every product must measure:

Revenue
− Direct Delivery Cost
− Infrastructure Cost
− AI and Model Cost
− Development and Maintenance Cost
− Implementation Cost
− Support Cost
− Customer Success Cost
− Marketing Cost
− Sales Cost
− Partner Commission
− Marketplace Fee
− Refund and Service Credit
− Allocated Operating Cost
= Product Contribution or Net Product Profit.

The official accounting treatment and formulas shall be maintained by the Finance authority and Enterprise KPI Dictionary.

## **99.3 Product-Level Financial Record**

Every product must maintain:

- Product ID;

- Revenue Owner;

- Cost Owner;

- revenue streams;

- direct costs;

- allocated costs;

- gross profit;

- contribution margin;

- net profit;

- gross margin percentage;

- net margin percentage;

- breakeven point;

- cumulative investment;

- payback;

- ROI;

- cash collection status;

- forecast;

- confidence level;

- review date.

## **99.4 No Hidden Cost**

Costs shall not be excluded merely because they are paid by another Daffodil entity.

Shared resources must be attributed through an approved allocation method.

## **99.5 Institutional Product Return**

Products built primarily for internal use shall calculate institutional return through:

- cost avoided;

- time saved;

- manpower optimized;

- error reduced;

- risk reduced;

- revenue protected;

- student outcome improved;

- compliance improved;

- service quality improved;

- decision speed improved.

# **CHAPTER 103**

# **MRR, ARR AND RECURRING REVENUE GOVERNANCE**

MRR shall include eligible recurring subscription value normalized to a monthly basis.

It shall exclude:

- one-time implementation;

- hardware;

- non-recurring training;

- one-time customization;

- taxes;

- pass-through charges;

- unapproved or uncollectable amounts.

## **103.2 Annual Recurring Revenue**

ARR shall represent eligible recurring value normalized annually.

Official definitions shall remain in the Enterprise KPI Dictionary.

## **103.3 Recurring Revenue Movements**

Track:

- New MRR;

- Expansion MRR;

- Contraction MRR;

- Reactivation MRR;

- Churned MRR;

- Net New MRR.

## **103.4 Recurring Revenue Integrity**

No annual contract shall automatically be treated as recurring if renewal is improbable, contractually non-renewable or dependent on extraordinary customization.

## **103.5 Recurring Revenue Concentration**

DAIOS shall monitor:

- revenue concentration by customer;

- revenue concentration by product;

- revenue concentration by industry;

- revenue concentration by country;

- revenue concentration by partner.

Excessive concentration shall trigger risk review.

# **CHAPTER 107**

# **BREAKEVEN, PAYBACK AND ROI**

Every product investment must define its expected financial recovery.

## **107.1 Breakeven**

Breakeven analysis shall consider:

- fixed investment;

- recurring fixed cost;

- variable cost;

- price;

- expected customers;

- expected usage;

- margin.

## **107.2 Payback Period**

The expected time required to recover the approved investment must be estimated and reviewed.

## **107.3 Return on Investment**

ROI shall compare relevant financial return with total approved investment.

For institutional products, return may include quantified:

- cost avoidance;

- productivity gain;

- risk reduction;

- revenue protection;

- service improvement.

## **107.4 Confidence Level**

Every breakeven and ROI estimate must state:

- assumptions;

- data source;

- forecast period;

- confidence;

- risk;

- sensitivity.

## **107.5 Unrealized Return**

Products that repeatedly fail to approach approved return targets must undergo corrective or retirement review.
