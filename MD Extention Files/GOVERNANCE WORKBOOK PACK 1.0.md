# **DAIOS GOVERNANCE WORKBOOK PACK (GWP) 1.0**

## **Enterprise Governance, Execution Tracking, Control & Accountability Workbook Framework**

# **EXECUTIVE PURPOSE**

The DAIOS Governance Workbook Pack (GWP) is the operational control system of DAIOS.

Constitution defines:

### **Principles**

Operating Model defines:

### **Responsibilities**

RACI defines:

### **Accountability**

Workbook Pack defines:

### **Execution**

Without Governance Workbooks:

- Governance becomes theoretical

- Progress becomes invisible

- Risks become hidden

- Ownership becomes unclear

With Governance Workbooks:

Everything becomes measurable.
