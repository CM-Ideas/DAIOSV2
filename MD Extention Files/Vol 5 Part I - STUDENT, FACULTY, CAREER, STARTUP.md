# **CHAPTER 224**

# **HUMAN INTELLIGENCE CONSTITUTION**

## **224.1 Executive Principle**

Education shall not be managed only through:

- admission;

- attendance;

- examinations;

- grades;

- certificates;

- graduation.

DAIOS shall create a governed Human Intelligence Layer that understands the complete development of:

- students;

- faculty;

- graduates;

- alumni;

- mentors;

- founders;

- professionals;

- future leaders.

## **224.2 Human Intelligence Purpose**

Human intelligence shall support:

- learning;

- skills;

- wellbeing;

- employability;

- career;

- innovation;

- entrepreneurship;

- leadership;

- lifelong growth;

- institutional impact.

## **224.3 Human Accountability**

Human intelligence shall not reduce a person to a single score.

Every score must:

- have a defined purpose;

- use governed data;

- disclose relevant limitations;

- support human review;

- provide correction or appeal where appropriate;

- avoid unsupported sensitive inference.

## **224.4 Human Development Principle**

The objective is not only to measure people.

The objective is to help them improve.

# **CHAPTER 225**

# **ONE STUDENT ONE AI CONSTITUTION**

## **225.1 Executive Declaration**

Every eligible student shall receive access to a governed Personal AI capability appropriate to the student’s stage, programme, rights, needs and institutional context.

## **225.2 Purpose**

One Student One AI shall help the student:

- understand learning requirements;

- find trusted knowledge;

- identify skill gaps;

- plan learning;

- build a portfolio;

- explore careers;

- prepare for employment;

- develop products;

- explore entrepreneurship;

- receive mentoring;

- access student services;

- continue learning after graduation.

## **225.3 Personal AI Roles**

A student’s AI may operate as:

- Learning Assistant;

- Study Planner;

- Skill Coach;

- Research Assistant;

- Portfolio Assistant;

- Career Coach;

- Employability Coach;

- Innovation Assistant;

- Startup Assistant;

- Student Service Guide;

- Lifelong Learning Assistant.

## **225.4 Personalization**

Personalization may consider authorized:

- programme;

- courses;

- learning progress;

- skills;

- career goal;

- language;

- accessibility need;

- portfolio;

- interests;

- certifications;

- approved student preferences.

## **225.5 Personal AI Limitations**

The Personal AI shall not independently:

- award marks;

- alter academic records;

- determine progression;

- impose discipline;

- diagnose medical or psychological conditions;

- make binding employment decisions;

- disclose restricted student information;

- replace faculty responsibility;

- replace professional counselling where required.

## **225.6 Student Control and Transparency**

Students must be informed, where applicable, about:

- the purpose of the AI;

- what data it uses;

- what it can and cannot do;

- how to correct inaccurate information;

- how to request human support;

- how important recommendations are reviewed.

## **225.7 Personal AI Continuity**

The Personal AI shall grow with the student through governed lifecycle stages while respecting:

- privacy;

- access;

- data minimization;

- retention;

- consent or lawful authorization;

- role transitions.
