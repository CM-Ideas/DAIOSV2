- researchers;

- developers;

- designers;

- testers;

- royalty;

- revenue share;

- paid internship;

- apprenticeship allowance;

Commercial participation must not:

- compromise academic assessment;

- create unfair advantage;

- exploit compulsory student work;

- replace required learning outcomes;

- conceal conflicts of interest.

## **127.7 Student Revenue Experience**

- product experience;

- customer experience;

- marketing experience;

- sales experience;

- revenue experience;

- portfolio;

- entrepreneurial capability.
