# **DAIOS DECISION RIGHTS MATRIX (DRM) 1.0**

## **Daffodil AI Operating System**

### **Enterprise Decision Authority, Approval Governance & Escalation Framework**

# **1. EXECUTIVE PURPOSE**

The DAIOS Decision Rights Matrix (DRM) defines:

### **Who Has Authority To Decide**

### **Who Has Authority To Approve**

### **Who Has Authority To Reject**

### **Who Must Be Consulted**

### **Who Must Be Informed**

for every critical decision across the DAIOS ecosystem.

# **WHY DRM IS DIFFERENT FROM RACI**

## **RACI Answers:**

Who participates?

Who is responsible?

Who is consulted?

Who is informed?

## **DRM Answers:**

Who can actually say YES?

Who can actually say NO?

Who can authorize budget?

Who can approve risk?

Who can authorize release?

Who can authorize commercialization?

# **GOVERNANCE RULE**

RACI controls execution.

DRM controls authority.

Both are mandatory.
