# **CHAPTER 163**

# **ENTERPRISE INTELLIGENCE CONSTITUTION**

## **163.1 Executive Principle**

Data alone is not intelligence.

Reports alone are not intelligence.

Dashboards alone are not intelligence.

Intelligence is created when DAIOS converts:

Data
→ Context
→ Information
→ Knowledge
→ Explanation
→ Prediction
→ Recommendation
→ Decision
→ Action
→ Outcome
→ Learning.

## **163.2 Intelligence Purpose**

Enterprise intelligence must help DAIOS answer:

- What happened?

- What is happening now?

- Why did it happen?

- What is likely to happen?

- What could happen under different conditions?

- What risks require attention?

- What opportunities are emerging?

- What decision is required?

- Who must act?

- By when?

- What outcome is expected?

- Did the decision work?

## **163.3 Intelligence Is Not Reporting**

A report that merely summarizes data shall be classified as reporting.

Enterprise intelligence must provide one or more of:

- diagnosis;

- causation;

- comparison;

- anomaly detection;

- risk;

- opportunity;

- prediction;

- simulation;

- recommendation;

- decision requirement.

## **163.4 Intelligence Domains**

DAIOS intelligence shall operate across:

- Governance Intelligence;

- Product Intelligence;

- Architecture Intelligence;

- Delivery Intelligence;

- AI Intelligence;

- Security Intelligence;

- Data Intelligence;

- Knowledge Intelligence;

- Revenue Intelligence;

- Customer Intelligence;

- Marketplace Intelligence;

- Student Intelligence;

- Faculty Intelligence;

- Research Intelligence;

- Startup Intelligence;

- Alumni Intelligence;

- Talent Intelligence;

- Asset Intelligence;

- Infrastructure Intelligence;

- Sustainability Intelligence;

- Strategic Intelligence.

## **163.5 No Unsupported Intelligence**

No intelligence output may present unsupported assumptions as established facts.

Every material conclusion must identify:

- source;

- date;

- evidence;

- confidence;

- limitation;

- responsible owner;

- review requirement.
