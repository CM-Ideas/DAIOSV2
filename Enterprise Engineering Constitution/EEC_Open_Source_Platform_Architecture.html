Cross-cutting overlay — autonomous governance agent mesh

A supervisor agent (built on CrewAI/AutoGen, served via Ollama) subscribes to events from every layer above through Kafka, continuously asking: what's delayed, blocked, missing, or below KPI threshold — surfacing findings to Layer 6 dashboards without waiting for a human status report. This is the "Enterprise Autonomous Governance" operation running as infrastructure rather than a manual habit.

Every component listed is free, open-source, and self-hostable — no paid SaaS subscriptions or metered API keys are required anywhere in this architecture. Source: Enterprise Engineering Constitution.docx and the DAIOS/DUDOS/Vol 1 Constitution governance documents in this project.
